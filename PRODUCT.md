# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Excuse serves creators, operators, and small teams who need to turn prompts, stories, reference media, and existing audio/video material into usable AI content. Users work inside an authenticated production tool where they choose models, tune parameters, track generation records, manage credits, inspect Canvas pipeline progress, and recover or retry failed work.

## Product Purpose

Excuse is a full-stack AI content generation platform for text, image, video, story-to-film Canvas workflows, and ASR-based subtitle burning. Its promise is "let imagination have productivity": creative intent should become durable, inspectable production output rather than disposable one-off tasks.

Success means users can start from a prompt or story, understand what the system is doing, see cost and progress clearly, recover from provider or pipeline failure, and keep generated results as assets.

## Brand Personality

Practical, imaginative, dependable.

The product should feel like a capable creative production desk: clear enough for repeated daily work, expressive enough to make generation feel alive, and steady enough that users trust long-running AI jobs with their credits and media.

## Anti-references

This should not look like an untouched shadcn/ui starter, a generic grayscale AI SaaS dashboard, or a flashy landing-page toy that hides operational state. Avoid decorative color, vague "magic" visuals, and interfaces that make background work feel invisible or unrecoverable.

## Design Principles

- Make production state visible: progress, cost, failures, retries, and assets should be legible at a glance.
- Use color as meaning: brand color should guide primary action and creative category, while status colors remain consistent and accessible.
- Keep the tool in flow: common generation tasks should feel direct, dense, and recoverable.
- Let creativity show up in outcomes: the interface supports imagination without competing with generated content.
- Preserve trust under failure: long-running and provider-backed work needs honest feedback, stable state, and clear next steps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for text and interactive controls. Do not rely on color alone for status or category. Keep touch targets comfortable on narrow screens, support keyboard focus states, and respect reduced-motion expectations for product UI.
